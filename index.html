Comprehensive Heating, Cooling & Mechanical Services

We service, repair, and install all major residential and light commercial HVAC equipment makes and models—engineered specifically for Utah's high-desert elevation and seasonal extremes.

Winter Comfort

Heating Services

Reliable furnace and heating solutions to keep your family or business warm, safe, and energy-efficient through freezing Utah winters.

  • Furnace Repair & Diagnostics Ignitors, blower motors, flame sensors, limit switches, and airflow troubleshooting.
  • High-Efficiency Furnace Installation & Replacement Proper Manual J load sizing and multi-stage / variable-speed gas furnace upgrades.
  • Heat Pump Systems & Boilers Dual-fuel cold-climate heat pumps, hydronic heating, and radiant system service.
  • Seasonal Heating Tune-Ups & Safety Inspections Combustion analysis, carbon monoxide testing, and heat exchanger inspections.

Summer Relief

Air Conditioning Services

High-performance cooling repairs and modern system installations designed to handle triple-digit Wasatch Front summer afternoons.

  • Central A/C Repair & Emergency Troubleshooting Capacitors, contactors, refrigerant leak detection, frozen coils, and compressor care.
  • New Air Conditioner Installation & Upgrades High-efficiency SEER2 condensers, matched evaporator coils, and quiet operation.
  • Ductless Mini-Split Systems Targeted room-by-room cooling and heating for additions, garages, and bonus rooms.
  • Spring/Summer A/C Preventative Maintenance Condenser coil cleaning, refrigerant pressure checks, and condensate drain clearing.

Airflow & Controls

Indoor Air Quality, Ductwork & Controls

Eliminate hot and cold spots, combat Utah's dry air and seasonal inversions, and optimize your building's mechanical performance.

  • Smart Thermostat Installation & Zoning Controls Wi-Fi thermostats, motorized zone dampers, and multi-floor temperature balancing.
  • Whole-Home Humidifiers, Air Scrubbers & Filtration Steam/bypass humidifiers, UV-C germicidal purification, and high-MERV media filters.
  • Ductwork Repair, Sealing & Airflow Balancing Custom sheet metal transitions, return air additions, and static pressure optimization.
  • Light Commercial HVAC Service & Maintenance Rooftop package units (RTUs), commercial split systems, and scheduled filter/belt care.
